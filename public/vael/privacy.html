Privacy policy

Game “Cendres de Vael” — last updated: 14 November 2026 · Version française

In short: the game has no ads and no tracking tools. We never sell or rent any data. You can play without creating an account. If you create one, we only keep what is needed to save your game, show the leaderboard and restore your purchases.

1. Who is responsible

The game is published by 3WM Service (sole proprietorship, France). For any question about your data: service@3-wm.net.

2. Data we use

DataWhyWhere
Progress, settings and local saveTo run the gameOnly on your device
Email address and password (encrypted), or Google sign-inTo create and protect your account (optional)Account server (Supabase)
Online game saveTo get your hero back on another deviceAccount server (Supabase)

This data travels encrypted (HTTPS). We do not collect your GPS location, contacts, photos or your phone’s advertising ID.

3. Player chat

  • Chat only exists inside a multiplayer game (4 players max, brought together by a code).
  • Messages go through our server to reach the other players. They are neither stored nor read.
  • Swear words and links are hidden automatically.
  • Any player can ignore or report another. A report sends us, by email, that player’s nickname and last messages. We keep them while handling the report, 3 months at most.
  • Chat can be turned off in Settings › Player chat.
  • Never share personal information (name, address, phone number) in the chat.

4. How long

  • Account, save and purchases: as long as the account exists.
  • Leaderboard scores: reset every week. Old scores are deleted after 3 months at the latest.
  • Chat messages: never stored.
  • Payment data: kept by the payment provider according to its legal obligations.

5. Who we share with

Only with the providers that run the game: account hosting (Supabase), payment and, if you use it, Google sign-in. They process data on our behalf. No data is sold or used for advertising.

6. Deleting your account and data

Send an email to service@3-wm.net with the subject “Account deletion”, from the address linked to your account (or stating your nickname).

Within 30 days we delete your account, your online save, your scores and your purchase list. Payment records that the law requires to be kept are retained by the payment provider. Purchased items cannot be recovered after deletion.

To erase only the data on your device, uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone settings.

7. Children

The game is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. In France, a minor under 15 needs a parent’s consent to create an account. If you think a child has created an account without that consent, write to us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, and object to its use, by writing to service@3-wm.net. We reply within one month. You can also file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr), or the authority in your country.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated. For an important change, a message will be shown in the game.

In-game purchases are governed by the terms of sale (in French).
